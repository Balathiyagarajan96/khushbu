Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long does bridal blouse stitching take?

Usually 10 to 15 days. Express 7-day slots are available during the wedding season.

Can I get a trial fitting?

Yes. Every bridal order includes a trial fitting so we can perfect the fit before the final finish.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Free pickup and delivery within Chennai, and courier delivery across India.

Can I bring my own design?

Absolutely. Share reference photos and our designers will recreate or refine them for you.

Are alterations included?

Free alterations within 7 days of delivery on all stitched orders.
